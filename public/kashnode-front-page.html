How It Works

Order in. Verified. Parcel out.
Nothing in between is manual.

One order travels through four automated stages — from the Order Now button to a verified customer and a booked parcel — without your team touching it.

1

Order arrives via plugin

The KashNode plugin on your WooCommerce, Shopify or custom store takes the order — the customer fills in their phone number and taps Order Now, choosing bKash or Cash on Delivery.

Plugin · WooCommerce / Shopify / Custom
2

SMS verification from your SIM

The KashNode Box at your premises sends a verification SMS from your own business SIM. The customer enters the code — proof this is a real customer, so fake and fraud orders drop sharply.

Your SIM · Verification code
3

Payment is verified

Paying by bKash? The customer's payment is verified with its verification code / TrxID against the exact order and amount. Prefer COD? That works too — no prepayment needed.

bKash code check · COD supported
4

AI confirms & parcel ships

At order confirmation — or when the courier fulfilment request goes out — Kash calls the customer to say the order is confirmed, and the Steadfast / Pathao booking is created with a Rider Secret PIN.

AI confirmation call · Auto booking
The Verification System

Real customers get verified.
Fake orders stop at the door.

KashNode verifies people, not just payments. Your own SIM, sitting inside the KashNode Box at your home or office, is what talks to the customer — so every check comes from a number your customers already trust.

COD order? Verify by SMS first

Cash on Delivery is where fake orders hurt most. KashNode checks the customer before you pack anything:

1

Customer places the order

On your store, the customer enters their phone number and taps Order Now.

2

SMS goes out from your SIM

The KashNode Box — with your business SIM inside — sends the verification message to that number.

3

Customer enters the code

Only a real person holding that phone can complete it. Verified = a real customer.

4

You ship with confidence

Fake and fraud orders are filtered out before courier cost, packing time and return hassle.

Customer calls? The AI picks up

Customers never call an AI directly — they call your business number, exactly like always:

1

Customer dials your SIM number

The same business number printed on your page and parcels.

2

The KashNode Box forwards the call

From your SIM, the call is forwarded to the KashNode central server.

3

The server connects the AI

Kash takes the conversation from there — questions, order status, confirmation.

4

Zero human touch

And when an order is confirmed or handed to the courier, Kash calls out and tells the customer — automatically.

MFS payment — verified by code

The customer pays to your bKash, Nagad, Rocket or Upay number and submits the verification code. KashNode matches the code and TrxID to the order and amount before anything ships.

Cash on Delivery — fully supported

No prepayment? No problem. COD orders run through the same SMS verification, so you still know the customer is real before the rider goes out.

Meet Kash — The KashNode AI

Kash doesn't just read messages.
Kash talks to your customers.

Most stores lose orders because nobody picked up the phone. Kash answers every call, confirms the order, checks the payment — and speaks natural Bangla while doing it.

Listens — Speech to Text

Customer speech is transcribed live, Bangla and English mixed, even on a noisy phone line.

Thinks — LLM reasoning

Kash compares what the customer says against the order, the amount and the TrxID — and decides: verified, or needs a human.

Speaks — Text to Speech, your choice of voice

Replies go back as a clear voice on the same call — and you pick the voice: female or male, switchable anytime from your owner app. Confirmation, delivery update, or a polite follow-up — hands-free.
